About

I started in Human Resources, working in retail industries in the Philippines — compensation and benefits, payroll, recruitment, admin. Nothing to do with eCommerce.

I moved into freelancing almost by accident — or let's say I got inspired by someone to have the courage to do it. I started with sales and marketing in real estate, then transitioned to working with Amazon sellers and business owners, and I've been in it since 2021.

Since November 2025, I started learning AI automation on the side — n8n, Zapier, and some Google Apps Script. I trained under a small automation team for a while, but I'm learning it on my own now. There's still so much to learn, and I'm happy to always improve myself and upskill — whether it's learning advanced tech like AI, or relearning how things work from the ground up.

Though AI can help you build something quickly, I still believe it's better to know how things work from scratch.

Right now, I'd say we're lucky to have AI, because it helps us learn and relearn things on our own easily. I think that's the beauty of modern technology.
